# SHIDA BUSINESS TERMS OF USE

Version 1.0 — Working Draft for Legal Review

These SHIDA Business Terms of Use (“Business Terms”) govern the use of SHIDA professional functionality by a company, organization, institution, association or other entity (“Organization”).

SHIDA is developed and operated by NIHILOBA.

By creating or using an Organization on SHIDA, the person acting on its behalf confirms that they have reviewed these Business Terms and accept them where duly authorized to do so.

## 1. SHIDA Business

SHIDA Business enables Organizations to use modular professional functionality that may include recruitment, commerce, restaurants, hospitality, inventory, analytics, payments, school management and other SHIDA functionality.

An Organization activates only the modules and functionality available to it and needed for its operations.

Creating an Organization does not automatically provide access to every SHIDA module.

## 2. User and Organization

The Organization is distinct from the personal identity of the users who manage it.

A SHIDA user may belong to multiple Organizations.

Activities performed in a user's Personal SHIDA space remain separate from activities performed on behalf of an Organization.

## 3. Representation of the Organization

Any person acting on behalf of an Organization must be authorized to do so.

The Organization may designate an authorized representative to act on its behalf within SHIDA.

The authorized representative may be replaced using SHIDA's applicable governance and authorization mechanisms.

Authorized-representative status is separate from roles such as owner, administrator, recruiter or other internal roles.

Changing the representative does not automatically transfer Organization ownership, other roles or legal rights outside SHIDA.

## 4. Members and Permissions

Depending on available functionality, an Organization may authorize multiple users to act on its behalf.

The Organization is responsible for appropriately managing members, roles and permissions.

It must remove or modify access for persons who are no longer authorized to act for it.

SHIDA may revalidate authorization before sensitive actions.

## 5. Organization Verification

SHIDA may request information or documentation to verify:

- existence of the Organization;
- certain administrative information;
- authority of a person to act on its behalf.

Verification may include professional email, registration documents or other information reasonably required.

Verification documents are not intended to be public.

SHIDA verification only indicates that certain information has been checked under the applicable process. It is not a commercial guarantee or endorsement of the Organization.

## 6. Organization Address and Information

The Organization is responsible for the accuracy of its information.

Internal administrative information must not automatically become public.

Addresses used for administration, verification or internal management may differ from addresses or locations intentionally published through particular marketplaces.

## 7. Free and Paid Modules

Certain functionality may be provided free of charge.

Other functionality may be provided through paid modules, subscriptions, usage charges, transaction fees or specific contracts.

No paid module or service should be activated without first presenting its price and applicable terms to the Organization.

The Organization is not required to activate every available module.

## 8. Ending a Paid Module

Where a paid module expires or is cancelled, associated functionality may be disabled.

Expiry of a module does not automatically delete all historical Organization data.

Retention remains subject to the Privacy Policy, applicable obligations and SHIDA's retention rules.

## 16. Security
