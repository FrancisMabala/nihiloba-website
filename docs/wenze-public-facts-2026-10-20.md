# WNZ-09 — category-aware public product facts

Cards and product details now show recorded condition and available sizes,
shoe sizes, storage or other supported options. Compact cards show up to three
labels plus the remaining count; details show all available options. Sold-out
options stay in the purchase selector's existing availability flow.

Only supplied, allowlisted facts are rendered. Missing/unspecified/unknown
condition values remain absent for older products. Public pages support EN/FR;
the existing seller forms retain EN/FR/LN/SW.

The Nihiloba palette, image-led layout, stable product links, favourites and
checkout controls are preserved. No schema or deployment configuration changes.
Discovery filters are tracked separately in WNZ-10.
