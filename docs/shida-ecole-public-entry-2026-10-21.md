# SHIDA École public entry — 21 October 2026

SHIDA now has a public École entry at `/shida/ecole`, with localized
`/fr`, `/ln`, and `/sw` variants. The main SHIDA page links to it. The
entry gives school operators a working route to the private SHIDA Business
dashboard and tells visitors truthfully that no School profile is public yet.
It is separate from the planned NIHILOBA Education learning initiative.

This is an entry page, not a School directory or publication feature. It does
not query private School records, display a draft, create an Organization,
authorize a staff member, collect an enquiry, or trigger WhatsApp delivery.
The verified school contact remains private and confers no management rights.
The canonical School publication gate still requires School verification,
Organization consent, exact public revision review, moderation where required,
and an explicit public serializer. No School is published by this change.

Validation: focused route and link checks, localized content checks, typecheck,
targeted lint, and production build. No new dependency, environment variable,
worker, template, Backend endpoint, or migration.
