# Personal Restaurant & Malewa Stock handoff — 15 October 2026

Status: **Local implementation; release Partial.** No deployment, production intake change, commit or push.

## Daily seller flow

Stock opens on a searchable list of physical quantity, unit, available quantity after reservations, optional low threshold, status and last movement. Out and low stock sort first and have filters. The seller selects an item to receive stock, record waste or correct the observed physical count. Each operation carries a new key and current balance revision; an uncertain response keeps the same key for retry, while a stale revision reloads the balance before a new action. Movement history shows time, reason, change and balance after each movement. Creation is a short panel opened by a button. First use, no results, unavailable service, lost access and inactive tracking each have distinct text. FR/EN/LN/SW layouts were exercised at 360, 820 and 1440 px.

The seller declares whether a new record tracks **ready-to-serve portions**, **packaged drinks or other sellable units**, or **ingredients and supplies**. The Backend retains its authoritative `sellable`/`ingredient` purpose and adds nullable `stock_kind`. Historical records stay unclassified until the seller explicitly classifies them in the item panel; neither names nor menu categories are used to guess. Classification is revision checked and creates no movement. Sellable kinds use whole count units; ingredients may use existing count or measured units. The Backend exposes the latest movement timestamp and an as-of movement balance. Its existing exact establishment access, entitlement, reservation, consumption and cancellation rules remain authoritative. Receiving or wasting stock does not create an expense. A plated sale does not deduct ingredients automatically.

Menu links remain explicit. The item detail explains when a tracked link makes a menu item unavailable because its available quantity is below the per-sale factor. An unlinked menu item has no measured balance. A link to a different stock record is not silently moved. Personal controls contain no employee assignment. Organization stock worker grants and the Business workspace were left intact. WhatsApp still uses its existing exact establishment, four-language, Back/Help, durable interactive offer, numbered fallback and stale action flow; it now names a declared stock kind when available and keeps a generic label for legacy items.
